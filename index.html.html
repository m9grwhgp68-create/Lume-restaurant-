Our story

A little corner
of Italy in Leeds.

Lume began with a simple idea: cook the food we grew up with, from scratch, every day. Pasta rolled in the morning, vegetables from Yorkshire growers, and a wood fire that runs from open to close.

The room is warm and low-lit, built for long dinners and good wine. Whether it's a Tuesday plate of cacio e pepe or a Saturday celebration, you're family while you're here.

— Marco & Elena, founders
